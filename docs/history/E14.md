## E14. What each construct costs in the kernel, and the `[Kernel_Optimized]` tag (2026-11-06)

A definition that the kernel evaluates in a certificate carries `-- [Kernel_Optimized]` above its
docstring when it is in the fast form of E6–E9 (`grep -rn "Kernel_Optimized" ModularSymbolsTau`).
No tag means "evaluated, and not in that form"; the reason is in the table below.

**The criterion** (every definition below, ours or the core's, counts):
* its loops are bare recursors, not structural recursion (`brecOn`) or well-founded recursion;
* no `let`, tuple, or structure projection;
* nothing from `Int`, `Fin`, Mathlib;
* inside a loop body, or in anything called from one: no instance arithmetic (`+`, `*`, …), `ite`,
  `&&`, `Nat.blt`, matcher; the `OfNat` literal is allowed;
* everything it calls is itself in this form.
What runs once per check (a top-level `&&`, say) is allowed. Meter A, per use inside a `Nat.rec` loop whose
step costs 31–37 k instructions (`Nat.add r (nat_lit 3)`, 20 000 or 1 500 steps, baseline subtracted):

| construct, against the fast form | extra instructions per use |
|---|---:|
| `OfNat` literal `3`, against `nat_lit 3` | +0.1–0.25 k (under 1 %) — allowed |
| `Nat.blt` against `Nat.ble` as the test of a `Bool.rec` | +9 k |
| `&&` against nested raw `Bool.rec` | +8.5 k |
| `let` | +7.8 k |
| tuple and projection (`(Prod.mk a b).1`) | +12.9 k |
| `r + 3` (`HAdd.hAdd`) against `Nat.add r 3` | +19 k (+60 %) |
| `if r ≥ 7` (`ite`, `Nat.decLe`) against `Bool.rec` on `Nat.ble` | +64 k (4.8× the raw test) |
| structural recursion against a bare recursor | 2.7× per element (E6) |

**`packNat`** (`03193ed`) is now a bare `List.rec`. Alone it is 12–32 % cheaper (64 to 4096 digits). In the
real checks it moves little: `e2StackCheck` 3.919 → 3.870 G at level 997, 0.355 → 0.346 G at 401;
`phiPkOk` unchanged at weights 4 and 6. Most of the cost is the shift and the add on a growing numeral.

**What was tagged.** A walk of the kernel environment from the 1108 `decide +kernel` proofs of the
13 + 4 + 3 levels and the two acceptance files reaches 261 definitions of `ModularSymbolsTau` and 265 of the
core; 59 are tagged (66 pass; type abbreviations such as `SRow` and structure accessors are not tagged).
The generated `Levels` definitions (the data, `LT := LTS.map …`) are not tagged: they are emitted.
Most of the rest are the E3 walks and the `…W` checks of weights 4 and 6, which still use pairs, `List.all`,
`List.map` and structural recursion.

**Where the kernel time is** (`trace.profiler` with `-DElab.async=false`, per `decide +kernel`, levels 997, 701, 401
of weight 2 and 37 of weights 4 and 6: 10.2 s of kernel time for 320 checks):

| checks with this head | share | what keeps the head from being tagged |
|---|---:|---|
| `checkOneQ` (weight-2 `hP<p>`, 74 checks) | 16.7 % | `sumSl` returns a pair (`oP`, `oQ`) |
| `checkPk`, `heckeRowsOn…`, `phiPkOk` (weights 4 and 6 `hHecke`, `hP`) | 17.8 % | pairs, `List.all`, structural recursion |
| `checkPkQ` | 12.3 % | `Nat.blt` in `cmpQ`, `allFitQ`; `sumSl`; the pairs of `qappl`, `qorbit` |
| `starCheckW`, `dualCheckW`, `rankCheckW`, `colsOk` (weights 4 and 6) | 22.0 % | pairs, `List.all`, `List.map` |
| `starCheckQ` | 5.6 % | `Nat.blt` in `relWQ` |
| `colStepOkQ` | 4.6 % | `List.getD`, `List.map`, `cond` in `stepPrev`, `stepCoeff` |
| `cuspWalkOk` | 3.1 % | `cuspWalk` is structural recursion |
| `dualStackQ` | 3.0 % | **tagged** |
| `e2StackCheck` | 2.6 % | `Nat.blt`, `List.all`, `ones` through `List.replicate` |

So checks whose functions are all tagged are 3 % of the kernel time of these levels. Three small
changes would tag the biggest of the `…Q` forms: a `sumSl` without a pair, `Nat.ble` in place of
`Nat.blt` in six helpers (`cmpQ`, `allFitQ`, `relWQ`, `colsLtQ`, `absBigQ`, `treeCheckQ`), and tuple-free `qorbit`.
That changes the tag, not the time by itself: the table above prices each construct at 8–13 k per use.

**Caveats.** The walk is static: a definition counts as evaluated when a check mentions it, whether or not
a given run reaches it. Verdicts cascade (`calls X` in the report means a callee failed). Times are wall
clock on one machine; with async elaboration on, a glue theorem waits for the check it uses and shows the same time
(`hStep11` 2.6 s next to `hStepQ11` 2.6 s, against 0.0002 s and 0.014 s with it off), so only the synchronous times
mean anything. The scripts (environment walk, classifier, tagger, profiler) are not in the repository.

**Since 2026-11-21 the forms are compiled, not written by hand.** Every tagged definition is a
`kernel_def` (`Compute/KernelForm.lean`): the source is an ordinary definition in notation, and
the compiler produces the form of E6–E9 under the same name, with an equation the kernel checks.
The tag itself was then dropped, at the user's request: `kernel_def` marks those definitions,
and the criterion above applies to the kernel form, what `#print f` shows, not to the source. The
compiler changes notation and control flow (`&&`, `if`, `match`, `let`, `List.all`, structural
recursion), not representation, so a definition on pairs or `Int` carries
`-- TODO: kernel form: …` (pairs, an `Int`) in place of its `TODO` tag; those forms became
cheaper all the same, since their control flow is now in the
form of the table above (weights 4 and 6: 14 to 21 % less kernel time in `Basis` and `Hecke`).
The comparison of every generated term with the hand-written one it replaced, and the costs
before and after, are in `docs/bench/kernel_form/`.
